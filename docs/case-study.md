# FitCore Gym Management System

## Organisation and Business Scenario

FitCore Gym Management System is a simulated fitness business that provides gym membership services, fitness training, and equipment maintenance. The organisation requires a structured database to manage members, membership plans, subscriptions, payments, gym attendance, trainers, equipment, and maintenance requests.

## Database Users

The main users are gym administrators, receptionists, finance personnel, trainers, and facility officers. Each user group requires accurate and timely information to support its responsibilities.

## Major Data to Be Stored

The database will store member details, membership plan information, subscription periods, payment records, attendance records, trainer details, equipment information, and maintenance requests.

## Key Database Operations

The system will support member registration, membership creation and renewal, payment recording, attendance tracking, trainer record management, equipment registration, maintenance reporting, and operational reporting.

## Project Objective

The objective is to design a relational database that maintains data integrity, minimises unnecessary duplication, supports efficient queries, and provides a foundation for future optimisation and advanced database exercises.
